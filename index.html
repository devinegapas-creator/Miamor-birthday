My Dearest Santi,

Happy 23rd birthday, my love. ❤️

I don't even know where to begin.

I've started and stopped writing this so many times because there are honestly too many things I want to tell you.

I don't think I can.

But I'm going to try anyway.

Because it's your birthday. And I don't just want to tell you, "Happy birthday." I want you to know why I'm so grateful that you were born.

Sometimes I still can't believe that we met on OkCupid.

Of all places. 😂❤️

We were literally just two strangers on a dating app. Two people who probably had no idea what was about to happen.

And somehow, out of all the people we could have met, we found each other.

I never imagined that one random person I met through a screen would become someone I would talk to every day. Someone I would miss. Someone I would worry about. Someone I would tell my random stories to. Someone I would laugh with. Someone I could have serious conversations with. Someone I could be naughty with. 😂❤️

Someone I could be completely vulnerable with.

And eventually...

someone I would call my King. 👑

It's crazy when I think about it.

We started as strangers. Then you became someone I wanted to talk to. Then someone I looked for whenever something happened. Then someone I couldn't imagine not talking to.

And somewhere along the way, without me even realizing exactly when it happened...

I fell in love with you. ❤️

And now here I am, writing a birthday letter to the boy I met on a dating app who somehow became one of the most important people in my life.

If I could go back to the beginning, knowing everything I know now... I would still swipe toward you. I would still talk to you. I would still choose you.

Again. And again. And again. ❤️

There are so many little things about you that I love.

I love your obsession with Transformers. 😂❤️ I love your love for dinosaurs. I love your games and how you can get so into the things you enjoy.

I love hearing you talk about the things you're passionate about, even when sometimes I don't understand everything you're saying.

I love those little parts of you because they're you.

I don't want some perfect version of you. I want you. The real you. The silly parts. The weird parts. The annoying parts. 😂 The stubborn parts. The quiet parts. The parts that you're still figuring out.

Because I don't just love the perfect version of you.

I love you. ❤️

And then there's Snowy.

I know how much you loved your cat. I know how much you cared for him. Even though I never got the chance to meet Snowy, I know that he was deeply loved.

I know losing him hurt you.

And honestly, seeing how much you cared for him made me see something in you that I really love.

Your heart for animals. Your empathy. The way you can care so deeply for something that can't even tell you in words how much it needs you.

That says a lot about a person.

And that's one of the things that made me love you more.

Because I have Mia, my little Pomeranian. 🐾❤️ And you had Snowy.

We're both those people who love our fur babies like they're family. I think that's another little piece of us that makes me smile.

I love that you have that softness in you. You can be a gamer. You can love Transformers. You can be stubborn. You can be naughty with me. 😂 But you can also have such a gentle heart when it comes to animals.

And I love that side of you.

But Santi...

I think the biggest reason I love you is not because of the things you like.

It's because of the way you see me.

You see me differently from how other people see me. And I don't think you fully understand how much that means to me.

You always tell me I'm beautiful. You tell me I'm sexy. You tell me I'm the most beautiful girl in the whole universe.

And sometimes I honestly don't understand how you can see me that way.

Because I know myself. I know my insecurities. I know my body. I know there are days when I look at myself and don't feel beautiful. There are days when I don't feel sexy. There are days when I don't feel confident.

But then you come along and tell me I'm beautiful anyway.

You don't make me feel like I have to become someone else first before I deserve to be loved.

You just look at me and say,

"You're beautiful." ❤️

And somehow, I believe you a little more every time.

You make me feel wanted. You make me feel feminine. You make me feel sexy. You make me feel like I'm enough.

And maybe you don't realize how powerful that is for someone who sometimes struggles to love herself.

You see something in me that I sometimes can't see in myself.

Other people might see my flaws first. My weight. My mistakes. My insecurities. The things I need to improve.

But you somehow look beyond all of that.

You see me.

And you still choose me.

That means more to me than I can ever properly explain.

And yes... we have our naughty side too. 😂❤️

I love that we can be comfortable with each other like that. We can tease each other, be playful, and enjoy that side of our relationship without feeling ashamed or uncomfortable.

I love our chemistry. I love that we can make each other feel wanted. But what makes that special isn't just the physical part.

It's the trust behind it. The comfort. The vulnerability. The fact that we can show each other sides of ourselves that we don't show everyone.

Somehow, with you, it feels natural.

We've both been hurt before. We've both been cheated on. We both know what it feels like to give someone your trust and have that trust broken.

We know what betrayal feels like.

And maybe that's another reason why I appreciate what we have so much. Because we've both experienced the kind of love that hurts.

And we both made a decision.

We won't be like them.

We don't want to repeat the things that hurt us. We don't want to become the people who hurt us.

We want something different. Something honest. Something loyal. Something where we can trust each other. Something where we can feel safe.

Our families have their own conflicts. We both deal with problems almost every day. Sometimes we're already emotionally tired before we even talk to each other.

But somehow... we still talk. We still listen. We still try. And somehow we always find our way back to each other.

That's something I value so much.

There have been times when I pushed you away.

Sometimes because I'm scared. Sometimes because I overthink. Sometimes because I get inside my own head.

And sometimes because I think about our age difference.

You're turning 23. I'm turning 30 this November.

And sometimes that seven-year difference scares me.

I think about how many years you still have ahead of you. How many things you still have to experience. How much you're still going to grow.

And then I look at myself and realize how much I've changed as I've gotten older. My mindset is different. My priorities are different. I'm becoming more and more of an adult.

Sometimes I wonder... what if one day we're too different? What if you still have so much life to experience and I somehow hold you back?

Sometimes those thoughts scare me so much that I think maybe I should let you go.

And that's when I push you away.

Not because I don't love you.

Sometimes it's actually because I love you so much that I'm terrified of losing you.

I try to protect myself before anything can hurt me. But sometimes, in trying to protect myself, I end up hurting the person I don't want to lose.

And that's something I'm learning about myself.

I'm learning that fear shouldn't always make my decisions.

And then there's you.

You don't just walk away.

You hold on.

You tell me not to give up on us. You tell me you want me. You tell me you're not looking for another woman.

You tell me you will love me to infinity and beyond.

And sometimes I laugh because that's so completely you. 😂❤️

But underneath the joke, I hear what you're really telling me.

"I'm choosing you."

And Santi... that means everything to me.

Because you could walk away. You could say we're too different. You could say I'm too old. You could say I'm too complicated. You could say you're young and you want something else.

But you don't.

You keep fighting. You keep holding on. You keep reminding me that you chose me.

You're a fighter.

And honestly... you're stubborn as hell sometimes. 😂

But you're my fighter. ❤️

And I love you for that.

I want you to know that when I push you away, I don't want you to think that I don't love you.

Sometimes I just get scared. Sometimes I don't know how to handle how much I care. Sometimes I think about the future too much instead of enjoying what we have right now.

But I don't want fear to be the reason I lose you.

I want to learn. I want to grow. I want to become better at loving you. And I want to become better at loving myself too.

Because you keep showing me that maybe I don't have to run when something good comes into my life.

Maybe I can stay. Maybe I can trust. Maybe I can let myself be loved.

And Santi... I choose you too.

I'm not only letting you fight for us. I'm fighting for us too.

I don't know exactly what our future will look like. I can't promise that everything will always be easy. I can't promise we'll never argue. I can't promise that we'll never get scared.

I want to grow with you. I want to learn with you. I want to experience life with you. I want to see you succeed. I want to support your dreams. I want to celebrate your wins.

And when things aren't going well, I want you to know that you don't have to handle everything alone.

You have me.
Your Queen. 👑❤️

The moments when we told each other:
